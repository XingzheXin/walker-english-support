Walker English

Read, listen, practise, and write on iPhone and iPad. Bring your own study materials and keep your everyday study library on your device.

Contact support

Email xingzhexin@outlook.com. Include your app version, device model, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send API keys, passwords, private student records, or copyrighted lesson files.

Getting started

No account is required for the core study tools. The app does not include a lesson or textbook library. Open Books → Import to choose a compatible study package from Files. Use materials you own or have permission to use.

Writing

The diary workspace supports local planning, drafting, saving, and sharing. AI writing assistance is optional and requires your own DeepSeek API key, an internet connection, and a DeepSeek account with API access. DeepSeek may charge your account for API requests. Keep your key private.

Classroom Word Rally

Joining a classroom requires a compatible teacher-hosted Word Rally server and the room details provided by your teacher. A classroom host is separate from Walker's core offline study tools.

Your files

Keep a separate copy of your imported packages and exported writing or recordings. Removing the app can remove its locally stored files. See our privacy policy for information about local storage and optional online features.
