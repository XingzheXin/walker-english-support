Privacy policy

Walker English · Last updated October 4, 2026

Walker English is developed by Xingzhe Xin. This policy describes how the iPhone and iPad app handles information. For privacy questions, contact xingzhexin@outlook.com.

Study data stored on your device

Imported lesson files, audio and video, reading progress, study history, writing drafts, AI results you save, recordings, and preferences are stored on your device. Core reading, listening, recording, and local writing do not require a Walker account or upload this library to a developer-operated cloud server. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Device backups and files you place in cloud storage are governed by the settings and policies of the services you choose.

Optional DeepSeek writing assistance

If you enter your own DeepSeek API key, give permission in Optional AI Settings, and request an AI writing action, the app sends the text needed for that action directly to DeepSeek over HTTPS. Depending on the action, this may include your topic, title, planning choices, a sentence, surrounding diary text, a passage, and revision instructions. The API key is sent to DeepSeek to authenticate the request and is stored in your device's Keychain. These requests do not pass through a Walker server.

DeepSeek receives the submitted text, API authentication information, and connection information such as your IP address. Its handling, retention, and any use of that data are governed by DeepSeek's privacy policy and the terms of your DeepSeek account. Walker cannot delete data already retained by DeepSeek or promise that it will not be used for model improvement. Avoid submitting confidential information or personal information about other people. You can use the local diary workspace without an API key and withdraw permission or clear your key in Optional AI Settings to stop further AI requests.

Optional classroom connections

If you join a Word Rally classroom, the app connects to the teacher's host address. It sends the display name you choose, room credentials or a saved player token, your answers and drafts, and game actions needed for participation. The classroom host processes participation and results and may retain them. Your device also stores classroom bookmarks, credentials, and reports for resuming and reviewing a session. Ask the classroom operator about access, retention, and deletion of host records. Merely using the offline library does not join a classroom.

Permissions and sharing

Microphone access is used when you choose to record reading practice. A connected camera may supply a microphone; Walker records audio only and does not take photos or record camera video. Files access is used for materials you select to import or export. Local network access supports classroom connections. Photo-library permission may be requested when you choose to save an exported image. You control these permissions in iOS Settings.

When you use an export or share command, the selected material is passed to the destination you choose. That destination handles it under its own privacy policy. External dictionary or service links are handled by the app or website you open.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you include and uses them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle requests and maintain relevant support records. You can ask for its deletion using the address above, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove imported files and recordings using the app's management tools, and move writing to Trash and permanently delete it using the writing workspace. Removing the app removes its local app data, although separate exports, backups, and records held by an AI provider or classroom host may remain. Clearing a DeepSeek key prevents new AI requests but does not delete past requests held by DeepSeek. Contact each external service or classroom operator for their deletion process.

Children and classroom use

The app is a general study tool. Parents, guardians, and teachers should supervise children's use of online AI services and classroom connections. Core offline tools do not require a child's name or contact details. Avoid using real student names in public rooms or sending identifiable student information to AI services.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
